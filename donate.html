The Mission

The Mechanical Bible is a free, open tool for decoding ancient Hebrew letter by letter. Every word is clickable. Every letter traced back to its Proto-Sinaitic pictograph. 58,400 Hebrew words analyzed. 31,143 verses. 66 books.

This project exists because the original meanings of these ancient words have been obscured by centuries of translation. The mechanical method strips away interpretation and reveals what the letters themselves say.
