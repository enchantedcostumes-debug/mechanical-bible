וימלך יהושפט בנו תחתיו ויתחזק עלישראל
His son Jehoshaphat succeeded him as king, and took firm hold of Israel.
II Chronicles 17:2 Gematria: 3825
ויתןחיל בכלערי יהודה הבצרות ויתן נציבים בארץ יהודה ובערי אפרים אשר לכד אסא אביו
He stationed troops in all the fortified towns of Judah, and stationed garrisons throughout the land of Judah and the cities of Ephraim that his father Asa had captured.
II Chronicles 17:3 Gematria: 2270
ויהי יהוה עםיהושפט כי הלך בדרכי דויד אביו הראשנים ולא דרש לבעלים
GOD was with Jehoshaphat because he followed the earlier ways of his forefather David, and did not worship the Baalim,
II Chronicles 17:4 Gematria: 2257
כי לאלהי אביו דרש ובמצותיו הלך ולא כמעשה ישראל
but worshiped the God of his father and followed the commandments—unlike the behavior of Israel.
II Chronicles 17:5 Gematria: 2677
ויכן יהוה אתהממלכה בידו ויתנו כליהודה מנחה ליהושפט ויהילו עשרוכבוד לרב
So GOD established the kingdom in his hands, and all Judah gave presents to Jehoshaphat. He had wealth and glory in abundance.

In the third year of his reign he sent his officers Ben-hail, Obadiah, Zechariah, Nethanel, and Micaiah throughout the cities of Judah to offer instruction.

With them were the Levites, Shemaiah, Nethaniah, Zebadiah, Asahel, Shemiramoth, Jehonathan, Adonijah, Tobijah and Tob-adonijah the Levites; with them were Elishama and Jehoram the priests.
II Chronicles 17:9 Gematria: 2315

They offered instruction throughout Judah, having with them the Book of the Teaching of GOD. They made the rounds of all the cities of Judah and instructed the people.
II Chronicles 17:10 Gematria: 3239
ויהי פחד יהוה על כלממלכות הארצות אשר סביבות יהודה ולא נלחמו עםיהושפט
A terror of GOD seized all the kingdoms of the lands around Judah, and they did not go to war with Jehoshaphat.
II Chronicles 17:11 Gematria: 7223

From Philistia a load of silver was brought to Jehoshaphat as tribute. The Arabs, too, brought him flocks: 7,700 rams and 7,700 he-goats.

Jehoshaphat grew greater and greater, and he built up fortresses and garrison towns in Judah.
