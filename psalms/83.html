Psalms 83:1 Gematria: 974
שיר מזמור לאסף
A song, a psalm of Asaph.
Psalms 83:2 Gematria: 2037
אלהים אלדמילך אלתחרש ואלתשקט אל
O God, do not be silent;do not hold aloof;do not be quiet, O God!
Psalms 83:3 Gematria: 1545
כיהנה אויביך יהמיון ומשנאיך נשאו ראש
For Your enemies rage,Your foes assert themselves.aassert themselves Lit. “lift up the head.”
Psalms 83:4 Gematria: 1584
עלעמך יערימו סוד ויתיעצו עלצפוניך
They plot craftily against Your people,take counsel against Your treasured ones.
Psalms 83:5 Gematria: 1735
אמרו לכו ונכחידם מגוי ולאיזכר שםישראל עוד
They say, “Let us wipe them out as a nation;Israel’s name will be mentioned no more.”
Psalms 83:6 Gematria: 1690
כי נועצו לב יחדו עליך ברית יכרתו
Unanimous in their counselthey have made an alliance against You—

the clans of Edom and the Ishmaelites,Moab and the Hagrites,

Gebal, Ammon, and Amalek,Philistia with the inhabitants of Tyre;

Assyria too joins forces with them;they give support to the descendants of Lot.bthe descendants of Lot I.e., the Moabites and the Ammonites; cf. Gen. 19.36–38.    Selah.

Deal with them as You did with Midian,with Sisera, with Jabin,at the brook Kishon—

O my God, make them like thistledown,like stubble driven by the wind.
