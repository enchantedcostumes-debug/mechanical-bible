What is the Red Letter Bible?

Traditional "red letter" Bibles highlight the words spoken by Jesus in red ink. This practice began in 1899 when Louis Klopsch, editor of The Christian Herald, was inspired by Luke 22:20: "This cup is the new testament in my blood, which is shed for you."

The Mechanical Bible's Red Letter edition goes further - showing each word Yeshua spoke in its original Hebrew and Greek context, with full pictographic analysis of every term.
