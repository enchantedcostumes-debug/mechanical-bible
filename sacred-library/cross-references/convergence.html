Convergence Analysis

Statistical analysis of patterns across traditions

Methodology

This analysis compares sacred texts from 7 independent traditions spanning 3,000+ years and 6 language families. Convergence is measured by:

  • Frequency of number appearances in key verses
  • Semantic similarity of associated concepts
  • Structural parallels (chapter counts, verse organization)
  • Gematria/numerology value distributions

Interpretation Note

Convergence does not prove common origin or divine authorship. Possible explanations include:

  • Universal human cognition patterns
  • Mathematical properties of small numbers
  • Cultural transmission along trade routes
  • Astronomical observations (lunar cycles, seasons)
  • Shared underlying reality (metaphysical hypothesis)

This analysis presents data without asserting any single explanation.
