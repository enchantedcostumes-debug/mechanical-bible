Mathematical Patterns

Why These Numbers?

Many divine numbers share mathematical properties:

  • 3, 7 - Prime numbers (indivisible)
  • 12 - Highly composite (1,2,3,4,6,12 divide it)
  • 72 - 8x9 or 6x12 (products of sacred numbers)
  • 108 - 27x4 or 12x9 (products of sacred numbers)
  • 144 - 12 squared (governance perfected)
