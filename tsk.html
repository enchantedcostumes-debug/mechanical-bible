What is the Treasury of Scripture Knowledge?

The Treasury of Scripture Knowledge (TSK) is the most exhaustive cross-reference system for the Bible ever compiled. Originally published in 1836, it contains over 500,000 cross-references linking related verses throughout Scripture.

The Mechanical Bible integrates TSK with Hebrew pictographic analysis, showing not just that verses connect, but why they connect through shared Hebrew root words and concepts.

History of the TSK

The Treasury of Scripture Knowledge was compiled by R.A. Torrey and published in 1836. It built upon the work of earlier scholars, particularly the marginal references in the 1611 King James Bible and the cross-reference work of John Brown of Haddington.

Unlike a concordance (which lists where words appear), the TSK shows conceptual connections - verses that illuminate each other's meaning even when they don't share exact vocabulary.
